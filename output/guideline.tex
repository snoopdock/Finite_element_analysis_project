The finite element method (FEM) is a numerical technique used to solve partial differential equations (PDEs) by discretizing the domain into smaller elements. The FEM relies on the weak formulation of the PDE, which transforms the equation into an integral form by multiplying with a test function and integrating over the domain. This approach is central to the FEM as it enables the use of variational principles and ensures compatibility with numerical discretization. The weak form is typically expressed as a bilinear equation involving a bilinear form \(a(u, v) = L(v)\), where \(u\) is the solution in the Hilbert space \(V\), \(v\) is a test function, and \(L(v)\) is a bounded linear functional.
