# AE-29 Transition Hardening in G9.32

## Finding

The G9.31 implementation correctly required a complete chain inside
`evaluate_iteration`, but the public `advance_session` function accepted a
validly hashed decision record without independently rechecking the evidence
that justified that decision.

That allowed a caller to construct an `accept_repair` decision with no repair,
verification, or post-audit and advance the session.

## Correction

G9.32 adds `_validate_transition_result()` and makes `advance_session()` a
second integrity boundary.

The transition now revalidates:

- controller-result envelope;
- exact input snapshot;
- decision identity and iteration;
- audit report;
- neural handoff;
- repair and finding bindings;
- candidate snapshot derived from the repair;
- verification receipt;
- post-repair audit;
- decision/state-change consistency;
- accepted-repair completeness.

An `accept_repair` transition is now impossible without the complete repair,
passed verification, candidate state, and post-audit chain.

## Compatibility

Valid G9.31 scenarios continue to pass. The change does not authorize
authoritative-checkout mutation and does not change the decision-memory
authority boundary.

## Remaining trust boundary

The verification receipt remains an external input. AE-29 validates its
structure, digest, snapshot binding, repair binding, and authority flags, but
does not cryptographically authenticate the external verifier itself.

That is intentionally recorded as a prerequisite for later unattended
automation rather than hidden inside AE-30.
